# Design System: Mars Bank

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Orbital Registry, Account Desk"**

Mars Bank has two deliberate, current visual worlds. Public and login surfaces retain the Orbital Registry: a dark graphite coordinate sheet or light dust paper, condensed Saira typography, ruled cells, and survey-like precision. Authenticated routes under `/banking` use the Search-led Account Desk: a light operational workspace with graphite chrome, white ledger sheets, Hanken Grotesk, and restrained rounded geometry.

These are not themes of one surface and the desk does not replace the public identity. The shared brand constants are the orbital-arc lockup, oxide action color, and signal blue. The route boundary is the authority: public styles inherit root theme tokens; authenticated desk tokens are scoped to `.banking-shell` and stay light in both themes.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two route-bound worlds: expressive Orbital Registry in public, quiet Account Desk after sign-in
- Orbital lockup, oxide, and signal blue connect both worlds
- Public uses graphite/dust, condensed Saira, sharp survey rules; desk uses cool neutral sheets, Hanken Grotesk, 6px cards, and 3px controls
- Signal communicates financial availability; oxide communicates action
- Both worlds avoid game-like sci-fi decoration and preserve premium-bank credibility

## Colors

The public palette is mineral and theme-responsive; the desk palette is cool, light, and locally scoped. Graphite, oxide, and signal are shared.

### Primary
- **Oxide:** The cross-world action color and orbital node. Filled primary actions darken to oxide-dark on hover.

### Secondary
- **Signal:** Focus in both worlds. In the authenticated desk, signal has the narrower semantic role of spendable money and positive posted movement; it must not color loan debt, an original principal, credit owed, or a credit limit. Public light mode uses signal-dust where the darker value is required for contrast.

### Neutral
- **Public graphite / mint:** Default public ground and inverse type.
- **Public dust family:** Light-theme paper, soft surface, mineral edge, muted copy, line, and rule.
- **Desk canvas / sheet / shade:** Cool page field, ledger surface, and table-header or totals tint.
- **Desk ink / soft / faint:** Primary copy, metadata, and low-emphasis directional marks.
- **Desk rule / strong rule:** Ordinary separators and the stronger reconciliation boundary.
- **Desk chrome family:** Constant graphite top bar and rail, with dedicated ink, muted ink, divider, button, hover, and button-hover values.
- **Success / danger:** Confirmation and error states. Posted credits in the desk use signal, not success.

### Named Rules
**The Two Grounds Rule.** Public pages may switch between graphite and dust. Every authenticated desk stays on its light canvas in both themes while its top bar, rail, and mobile tabs stay graphite.

**The Spendable Signal Rule.** In the desk, signal blue marks money the customer can spend or positive posted movement. Loan debt, credit owed, original principal, and credit limits remain desk ink.

**The Action Oxide Rule.** Oxide means the primary action and the active rail edge. Do not turn it into a general surface fill.

## Typography

**Public Display Font:** Saira Extra Condensed (Arial Narrow fallback)  
**Public Body Font:** Saira (Helvetica Neue fallback)  
**Desk Font:** Hanken Grotesk Variable (Hanken Grotesk, then public body fallback)

**Character:** Public typography behaves like a survey instrument: condensed, often uppercase, tightly led, and deliberately tracked. The desk is calmer and sentence-case, with a compact ten-step scale and tabular financial figures.

### Hierarchy
- **Public display:** 52–92px clamp, 700, 0.88 leading for hero headlines.
- **Public headline:** 38–56px clamp for section headings; 42–66px clamp for page headlines.
- **Public figure / title / wordmark:** 58–88px figures, 28px titles, and 22px lockup type preserve the incumbent registry hierarchy.
- **Public body:** 16px / 1.5 for explanatory copy.
- **Desk micro:** 10px for the Demo data chip, mobile tab labels, and restated row words when the ledger compresses.
- **Desk XS / SM / Base / MD / LG / XL:** 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, and 16px for labels through account figures.
- **Desk 2XL / 3XL:** 18 and 21px for card headings and summary values.
- **Desk display / figure:** 32px for the greeting; 40px for large desk figures.
- **Desk labels:** 11px tracked uppercase is reserved for column headers, metric labels, and totals labels; 10px micro covers the Demo data chip, mobile tabs, and compressed restated row words. Ordinary navigation and prose remain sentence case.
- **Financial figures:** Use tabular numerals. Units belong in headers or compact suffixes rather than being repeated as visual noise.

### Named Rules
**The Route Type Rule.** Saira Extra Condensed gives public pages their registry voice; Hanken Grotesk gives authenticated work its desk voice. Do not carry public all-caps display typography into `/banking`.

**The Reserved Uppercase Rule.** In the desk, tracked uppercase belongs only to structural labels: column headers, metric labels, totals labels, and restated row words.

## Layout

Public IA remains Macquarie-like: a 36px utility row, 78px primary nav, and `min(1240px, calc(100% - 48px))` public width. The split hero, token-story images, and mint security plate sit in that same column, not the viewport. The security row keeps lock, copy, and login link as one group. The four-cell explore grid and three-column service band follow the column. It changes at 1100px, 850px, and 560px.

The authenticated shell is a 60px full-width top bar above a 118px sticky icon rail and a fluid stage. Register content is left-aligned to the rail, fills the stage up to 1180px, and uses 32px inline padding with 30px top and 64px bottom padding. It is not a centered column. The overview is a vertical 20px rhythm: greeting band, one account ledger, recent transactions, then the three-up sol summary. Account rows share six visible tracks: account, branch and number, available, held, total, and arrow.

At 1080px the desk content tightens. At 900px the customer control collapses to its avatar. At 850px the rail hides, the top bar wraps search to a full second row, the fixed four-tab mobile bar appears, and account rows preserve the available amount while hiding held and total. The 850px mobile transition is structural, not merely a scale reduction.

### Named Rules
**The Shared Column Rule.** Every visible account uses one common ledger grid. If a product changes the meaning of a shared column, keep the grid and restate the row's words.

**The Reconciled Total Rule.** The ledger totals row is the available total. It is computed from the spendable set: deposit balance excludes unused credit; loans count toward neither figure. The sol glance note uses that same spendable figure.

## Elevation & Depth

Both worlds are predominantly flat. Public structure comes from contrasting grounds and one-pixel survey rules. Desk depth comes from canvas, sheet, shade, and one-pixel rules; ledger cards do not use shadows. The physical bank-card depiction is the established exception, with `0 16px 28px rgba(16, 21, 24, 0.16)` because it represents an object rather than a content container.

Desk cards settle once on arrival over 380ms with `cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.68, 0.32, 1)`, beginning 6px below at zero opacity. Reading-order delays are 60ms and 120ms. The global reduced-motion rule collapses animation and transition durations to 0.01ms.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Sheet Rule.** Separate desk content with tone and rules, not elevation. Shadows remain reserved for depicted physical objects.

**The One Settle Rule.** Desk cards may perform the single arrival settle in reading order. Do not add looping, decorative, or competing motion.

## Shapes

Public registry containers, buttons, and fields remain square; 2px corners are reserved for depicted card objects and compact identity details. The desk uses gently rounded 6px ledger cards and compact 3px controls. Avatars and the orbital mark remain circular. One-pixel rules are the recurring structural edge in both worlds.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Public outline:** Square, transparent, one-pixel oxide stroke, mint type, 48px high; hover fills oxide and flips to graphite.
- **Public primary:** Square oxide fill with white type, 48px high; hover uses oxide-dark.
- **Desk primary:** 3px radius, oxide fill, white Hanken type, 46px high; the overview has one primary action.
- **Desk chrome controls:** 3px radius on graphite; muted dark fills progress to the dedicated hover fill.
- **Focus:** A 2px signal outline. Public offset is 3px; the desk-scoped offset is 2px.

### Chips
- **Demo data:** Persistent in every authenticated top bar, 2px radius, transparent graphite ground, subtle chrome border, muted chrome ink, 10px tracked uppercase. It is status, not a dismissible banner.

### Cards / Containers
- **Desk ledger card:** White sheet, one-pixel desk rule, 6px radius, clipped overflow, no shadow.
- **Desk shaded band:** The shade neutral distinguishes headers, explanatory totals, and notes without introducing elevation.
- **Public containers:** Ruled, square cells on graphite or dust.
- **Physical bank card:** 2px radius, 1.58 ratio, faint orbital ring, object shadow. The plastic face stays dark; status badges, freeze controls, and the security note beside it use desk ink on the light sheet.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Desk search:** White 38px field in graphite top bar, 3px radius, search icon, flexible input, and a rule-separated scope selector. Scope hides below 850px.
- **Public login:** Transparent bottom-rule field, square corners.
- **Focus:** Signal outline remains visible in both worlds.

### Navigation
- **Public:** Centered tracked uppercase links with a 2px oxide underline on hover.
- **Desk top bar:** 60px graphite band with orbital lockup, universal search, Demo data status, notifications, customer, and logout.
- **Desk rail:** 118px graphite, labels below 21px icons, 68px route rows, muted idle ink, graphite hover, white active ink, and a 3px oxide active edge.
- **Desk mobile:** Fixed four-tab graphite bar below 850px; active type is white while inactive type uses chrome-muted.

### Transfer
The send form is a desk sheet. Labels, fields, chips, and review copy use desk ink on the white sheet, not public paper or mint. Native controls inherit a light color-scheme so placeholders stay readable in both themes.

### Account Ledger
The account ledger is one sheet with shared columns. Transaction, savings, and offset accounts use Available / Held / Total. Credit rows restate Available credit / Owing / Limit; loan rows restate Remaining / Offset / Original. Restated words stay visible at every width. Only genuinely spendable available values receive signal blue. The strong-rule totals footer explains and reconciles the available and deposit figures.

### Orbital Lockup
The orbital-arc mark, oxide node, and signal ticks are shared across both worlds. Public lockup typography follows the public display family; the authenticated top bar pairs the mark with 15px Hanken Grotesk.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** choose the world by route: Orbital Registry for public/login, Search-led Account Desk for `/banking`.
- **Do** preserve the orbital lockup, oxide, and signal as cross-world brand constants.
- **Do** keep every authenticated canvas light in both themes and every authenticated chrome region graphite.
- **Do** use signal only for spendable or positive movement semantics in the desk.
- **Do** restate product-specific column meanings without breaking the shared ledger.
- **Do** keep the Demo data chip visible on every authenticated view.
- **Do** keep the ledger available total computed from the spendable account set.

### Don't:
- **Don't** present the Account Desk as a replacement for the public Orbital Registry.
- **Don't** carry condensed all-caps registry typography into authenticated daily work.
- **Don't** color loan debt, credit owed, original principal, or limits signal blue.
- **Don't** use sci-fi HUD glow, planet globes, or game chrome.
- **Don't** add kickers or eyebrows above headlines.
- **Don't** use shadows to lift desk ledger cards.
- **Don't** round public registry containers or square off desk cards to force one world onto the other.
