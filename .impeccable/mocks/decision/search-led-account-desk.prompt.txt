A pixel-crisp, full-fidelity desktop web UI concept mockup: the first viewport of the Mars Bank online banking dashboard at 1440x900, rendered flat and straight-on as a screenshot of a real browser viewport — no laptop frame, no device mockup, no perspective, no drop shadow, no browser chrome, no rounded page corners, edge to edge.

DIRECTION: "Search-led Account Desk" — a useful universal search bar and a familiar, generously spaced account table make the interface feel immediate, conventional, and calm. Calm density, plenty of white space, utilitarian and trustworthy like a serious retail bank's logged-in desk.

PALETTE, exactly these five: cool neutral canvas #F2F3F5 for the page ground, pure white #FFFFFF for the ledger surface, near-black #101518 for the utility chrome and primary text, oxide orange #E4623F for the single primary accent and active markers, coordinate blue #356CFF for plotted amounts, links and the available column. Materials: cool neutral canvas, white ledger surface, black utility chrome. Hairline 1px cool grey rules (#E1E4E8) separate table rows. No gradients, no glassmorphism, no glow, no space-theme decoration.

TYPOGRAPHY: one calm, soft humanist grotesk throughout, similar to Hanken Grotesk — rounded, friendly, generous x-height, normal width. Absolutely never condensed, never Saira Extra Condensed, never all-caps headings; sentence case everywhere except tiny 10-11px uppercase letter-spaced column labels. Numerals are tabular and right-aligned.
